The short verdict

Total Adblock is one of the easier ad blockers to set up: you install the browser extension and banners, pop-ups and video ads disappear across most sites straight away. In our use it handled YouTube and busy news pages well, and pages felt lighter. The main thing to weigh is not the blocking, which is solid, but the pricing. The low first-year offer renews at a much higher annual rate, so it best suits people who want a no-fuss blocker and will keep an eye on the renewal date.
